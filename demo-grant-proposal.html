Grant Proposal — Draft GrantsDAO / Sepolia

Open Grant Explorer

A public dashboard for tracking GrantsDAO's proposals, votes, and treasury activity — so anyone can see where community funds are going without running a node.

Requested $6,000
Milestones 3
Est. duration 6 weeks

Summary

Right now, understanding GrantsDAO activity means reading raw transaction logs or querying the contracts directly. This grant funds a public, no-login dashboard that surfaces proposal status, vote tallies, treasury balance, and grantee payout history in plain language — pulling directly from on-chain events, with no backend database to trust or maintain.

The goal is to lower the bar for community members who want to follow along or hold the DAO accountable, without needing to know how to read calldata.
